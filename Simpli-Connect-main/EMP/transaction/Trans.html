International Trasactions

International Transactions and Services Offered by Indian Banks

Foreign Currency Accounts

Employees can open foreign currency accounts in Indian banks to facilitate their international transactions. These accounts can be denominated in major currencies such as USD, EUR, GBP, and AED.

Remittance Services

Employees can send remittances to their families and friends in other countries through Indian banks. These services are typically offered at a discounted rate compared to other remittance providers.

Letters of Credit

Letters of credit are a type of financial instrument that is used to guarantee payment for goods or services that are being exported. Indian banks offer letters of credit to exporters to help them secure payment from their overseas buyers.

Foreign Exchange Trading

Employees can trade foreign currencies through Indian banks. This allows them to take advantage of favorable exchange rates and hedge against currency fluctuations.

Travel Cards

Indian banks offer travel cards that allow employees to make purchases in foreign currencies. These cards typically offer lower foreign exchange fees than credit or debit cards.

Other International Banking Services

In addition to the above services, Indian banks also offer a variety of other international banking services to employees:

  • International Wire Transfers: Employees can send wire transfers to overseas recipients through Indian banks.
  • International ATM Access: Employees can access their bank accounts from ATMs in other countries through Indian banks.
  • International Mobile Banking: Employees can use their mobile phones to access their bank accounts and make international transactions.

Eligible Employee Categories

Here are some of the employee categories that may be eligible for international transactions and services from Indian banks:

  • Expatriate Employees: Employees working in India but not citizens of India.
  • Traveling Employees: Employees frequently traveling to other countries for work or leisure.
  • International Students: Students studying in India who need to make international transactions for tuition, fees, and living expenses.

If you are an employee who needs to make international transactions, you should contact your bank to inquire about the specific services that are available to you.
