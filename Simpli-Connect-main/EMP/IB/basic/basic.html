Internet Banking Services Offered by Indian Banks

  • Account Information: Employees can view their account balances, transactions, and statements online.
  • Fund Transfers: Employees can transfer funds between their accounts and to other people's accounts online.
  • Bill Payments: Employees can pay their bills online, such as utility bills, credit card bills, and loan EMIs.
  • Chequebook Ordering: Employees can order chequebooks online.
  • Statement Download: Employees can download their account statements online.
  • Mini Statement: Employees can view their mini statements online.
  • Account Closure: Employees can close their accounts online.
  • Loan Applications: Employees can apply for loans online.
  • Investment Products: Employees can invest in investment products online.
  • Other Services: Employees can access a variety of other services online, such as setting up alerts, changing their passwords, and reporting lost or stolen cards.

Eligible Employee Categories

  • Permanent Employees: Employees with a regular, full-time job with a company. They may be eligible for internet banking services and other benefits from their employer.
  • Contract Employees: Employees working on a temporary or contract basis. They may not be eligible for internet banking services and other benefits from their employer.
  • Freelancers: Self-employed individuals without a regular employer. They may be able to access internet banking services on their own.

If you are an employee who is interested in internet banking services from an Indian bank, you should contact your bank to inquire about the specific options that are available to you.
