Salary Account: Features and Rules in Indian Banks

Features of Salary Accounts:

  • Zero Minimum Balance Requirement: This means that you don't have to worry about keeping a minimum balance in your account, which can save you money on monthly fees.
  • Free Transactions: Most banks offer a certain number of free transactions per month on salary accounts, such as ATM withdrawals, online transfers, and bill payments.
  • Free Debit Card: Many banks also offer a free debit card with salary accounts, which can be used to make purchases and withdraw cash from ATMs.
  • Other Benefits: Some banks also offer additional benefits with salary accounts, such as discounts on loans, free insurance, and access to exclusive banking services.

Rules for Salary Accounts:

  1. Account Opening: A salary account must be opened by an employer who has a tie-up with the bank.
  2. Eligibility: The account holder must be a salaried employee of the employer.
  3. Regular Salary Deposit: The account must receive a regular salary deposit every month.
  4. Minimum Balance: The account holder may be required to maintain a minimum balance in the account.

Additional Rules for Salary Accounts:

  • The maximum withdrawal limit from a salary account may be lower than the withdrawal limit from a regular savings account.
  • The number of free transactions per month may be limited.
  • There may be a penalty for non-maintenance of minimum balance.

It is important to read the terms and conditions of your salary account carefully to understand all of the rules and regulations that apply.
